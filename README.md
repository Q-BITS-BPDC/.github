## What is Q-BITS?

Q-BITS is the quantum computing student community at **BITS Pilani Dubai Campus**. We explore the intersection of quantum mechanics, linear algebra, and computer science — from foundational circuit theory to cutting-edge quantum machine learning and cryptography.

Whether you're a complete beginner curious about what a qubit actually is, or a CS/ECE student looking to build real projects with quantum hardware, Q-BITS is your launchpad.

> *"If you think you understand quantum mechanics, you don't understand quantum mechanics."* — Richard Feynman

## Workshop Tracks

Workshops are tiered by difficulty. Start anywhere that matches your background.

```
Track 0 — Foundations    no prerequisites
Track 1 — Algorithms     linear algebra + Track 0
Track 2 — Applications   Track 1 + basic ML
Track 3 — Hardware       Track 1 + some physics
```

## Resources

**Getting started**
- [IBM Quantum Learning](https://learning.quantum.ibm.com/) — free structured courses
- [Qiskit Textbook](https://qiskit.org/learn) — interactive, code-first
- [Quantum Country](https://quantum.country/) — spaced-repetition primer

**Going deeper**
- Nielsen & Chuang — *Quantum Computation and Quantum Information*
- [PennyLane Demos](https://pennylane.ai/qml/demonstrations/) — QML tutorials
- [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/list/quant-ph/recent) — latest preprints
- [Error Correction Zoo](https://errorcorrectionzoo.org/) — taxonomy of quantum codes
